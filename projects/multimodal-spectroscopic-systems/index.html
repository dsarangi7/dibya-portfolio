Multimodal Spectroscopic Systems

Eighteen months as a PhD trainee in ICFO's Medical Optics group, on laser-and-ultrasound thyroid screening with Hospital Clínic Barcelona. The measurement habits I built there are the ones I use on battery data today.

PhD trainee, July 2023 – January 2025. The PhD was not completed; I left by choice to move into energy. Certified by ICFO with a Certificate of Advanced Training in Photonics (May 2025).

The research

Thyroid nodules are common, and most are benign, so a cheap first screen matters. The group worked with Hospital Clínic Barcelona on the LUCA project: a point-of-care, low-cost device that combines diffuse optics with ultrasound to help screen thyroid nodules. I joined that work as a PhD trainee in Prof. Turgut Durduran's Medical Optics group.

What I did

I trained in diffuse optics and photon transport in biological tissue, and acquired data on laser- and ultrasound-based systems using diffuse correlation spectroscopy (DCS) and time-resolved spectroscopy (TRS). I built the processing and analysis workflows in MATLAB and R for multi-dimensional optical measurements.

Why I moved to energy

I chose to leave the PhD programme before completing it and move into the energy sector, to broaden my experience and take on a different kind of engineering problem. ICFO certified the period with a Certificate of Advanced Training in Photonics.

What carried over

Photon transport models became physics-based battery models. Noisy optical acquisition became noisy telemetry. The rule stayed the same: calibrate first, distrust a clean result, and write down the method. It is a large part of why I could deliver in marine batteries from the start.
