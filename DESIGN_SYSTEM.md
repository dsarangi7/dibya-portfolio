# Design System

Visual language:
- Dark background
- Neon blue, cyan, violet accents
- Glassmorphism panels
- Floating technical cards
- Animated grid background
- Subtle particles
- High contrast typography

Sections:
1. Hero
2. About
3. Engineering Timeline
4. Featured Projects
5. AI Systems
6. Battery R&D
7. Research Background
8. Skills
9. Contact

Avoid:
- Overloaded animations
- Childish gaming UI
- Fake claims
- Confidential company screenshots
